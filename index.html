Mining Tycoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Mining Tycoon is a mobile game published under the package name com.kuuhgle.miningtycoon. This policy explains what data the app stores or processes for gameplay, optional purchases, optional rewarded ads, cloud saves, and Android backup.

Data Stored By The App

The app stores game data so players can continue their progress:

  • cash, building unlocks, building levels, crew automation, boosts, contracts, prestige, and timestamps;
  • tutorial and help dismissed state;
  • purchase receipt ledger entries used to avoid granting the same purchase more than once;
  • cloud-save metadata such as last sync time when Play Games Saved Games is used.

Google Play Services

Mining Tycoon uses Google Play services for optional platform features:

  • Google Play Billing processes optional purchases and purchase restoration.
  • Google AdMob processes optional rewarded sponsor ads and ad reward events.
  • Google Play Games Saved Games can save or restore progress when the player uses cloud save features.
  • Android Auto Backup may preserve local app data for device restore when supported by Android.

Google may process device, account, purchase, diagnostics, advertising, and service interaction data under Google's own policies for these SDKs and services.

Purchases And Ads

Purchases and rewarded ads are optional acceleration features. The core game can be played without making purchases. Rewarded ads are shown only after the player chooses a sponsor boost.

Data The App Does Not Request

Mining Tycoon does not operate a custom account backend and does not request:

  • location from app code;
  • contacts, calendar, SMS, call log, health, or financial account data;
  • camera, microphone, photo library, or external storage access;
  • public chat, user-generated content, or social posting.

Data Sharing

Mining Tycoon does not sell personal data. Data may be processed by Google services when optional purchases, rewarded ads, Play Games Saved Games, or Android backup are used.

Deleting Data

The app does not create an app-owned account. Players can clear local app data through Android settings, uninstall the app, and manage Google Play Games or Google account data through Google's account controls.

Children

Mining Tycoon is a general audience strategy/idle game. It does not include public chat or user-generated content.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The updated date at the top of this page will change when the policy is revised.

Contact

For privacy questions about Mining Tycoon, contact the developer through the Google Play listing contact channel.
